The second note reads;

The entity seems to exclusively take an interest in human beings. Scientifically, human beings are more similar to anything else than they are to the entity. Visually, the entity's appearance resembles a human body more than anything else.

The third note reads;

It is bipedal and hairless, and it has a similar range of motion to that of human bodies. It has no facial features, no nose, no mouth, no eyes and no ears. Its skin has no blemishes, no pores, no wrinkles, no hair follicles. It has no orifices. Its entire body is like a stone smoothed by the ocean waves. Its body is shrouded in dark cloth that blends in with the shadows that surround it.

The fourth note reads;

It always appears in dark places full of shadows where it is hard to see. A light source may still illuminate parts of its body, such as the face, the torso and the limbs, but it will never allow any light source to illuminate its entire body at once. Everyone who encounters the entity disappears and is never heard from again.

The fifth note reads;

In photographs, it almost always appears as a white smudge that may sometimes resemble a humanoid shape. Its existence is known of by very few.

The sixth note reads;

Those who do know of the entity only learn of its existence through notes that describe it. The notes appear in random places at random times and have no known origin.

The seventh note reads;

Everyone seems to struggle with remembering that the entity exists when not directly looking at it, a photo of it, or one of the notes connected to it.

The eighth note reads;

Scientists have been unsuccessful in containing and studying the entity. They have also all forgotten it. They cannot protect you from it, no matter how much they try.

The ninth note reads;

These notes always appear in the same sequence. The next note in the sequence will only appear if somebody reads the previous note. It must always be the same person reading through the entire sequence of notes from first to last. Only the first note is visible to everybody, each subsequent note is only visible to whoever read the first note in the sequence. If nobody reads the first note, the second one will not appear, if nobody reads the second note, the third note will not appear, and so on, until the final note. After some time of remaining unread, the previous note will disappear and reappear somewhere where the person who started reading the sequence of notes is likely to find it. If they do not read the next note in the sequence, even after several reappearances, the notes will stop appearing to this person altogether and the first note will reappear somewhere else completely random where anyone will be able to read it. The number of times that a certain note will appear before disappearing completely will correspond to the number of that note in the sequence. For example, the fifth note will appear five times before it stops appearing and the first note starts appearing again. If someone continues reading each note in the sequence, they will collect all notes that are a part of the entity. But you've already figured this all out, haven't you?
