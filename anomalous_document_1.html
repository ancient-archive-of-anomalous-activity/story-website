The Nineteenth Note

These Notes Appear In Random Places At Random Times And Are Of Unknown Origin

The first note reads;

Wherever the number of trees
Is greater than three
It will appear
It tolls for thee
You may try to hide
You may try to flee
But it will still find you
And you will be
Gone

The second note reads;

The entity seems to exclusively take an interest in human beings. Scientifically, human beings are more similar to anything else than they are to the entity. Visually, the entity's appearance resembles a human body more than anything else.

The third note reads;

It is bipedal and hairless, and it has a similar range of motion to that of human bodies. It has no facial features, no nose, no mouth, no eyes and no ears. Its skin has no blemishes, no pores, no wrinkles, no hair follicles. It has no orifices. Its entire body is like a stone smoothed by the ocean waves. Its body is shrouded in dark cloth that blends in with the shadows that surround it.

The fourth note reads;

It always appears in dark places full of shadows where it is hard to see. A light source may still illuminate parts of its body, such as the face, the torso and the limbs, but it will never allow any light source to illuminate its entire body at once. Everyone who encounters the entity disappears and is never heard from again.

The fifth note reads;

In photographs, it almost always appears as a white smudge that may sometimes resemble a humanoid shape. Its existence is known of by very few.

The sixth note reads;

Those who do know of the entity only learn of its existence through the notes that describe it. These notes appear in random places at random times and are of unknown origin.

The seventh note reads;

Everyone seems to struggle with remembering that the entity exists when not directly looking at it, a photo of it, or one of the notes connected to it.

The eighth note reads;

Scientists have been unsuccessful in containing and studying the entity. They have also all forgotten it. They cannot protect you from it, no matter how much they try.

The tenth note reads;

These notes are an invitation.

The eleventh note reads;

These notes are a warning.

The twelfth note reads;

It spreads through being known. It is too late. The first was more than enough. Most people have disappeared by now. You are so, so lucky.

The thirteenth note reads;

We didn't want to go. We didn't want to kill them. Its persistent silence was deafening and suffocating and commanding. Obedience was choked out of me. I am frozen, and always will be.

The fourteenth note reads;

Even as I am aware of what I am doing to you, I cannot stop it, I am deeply sorry, I despise this fate.

The sixteenth note reads;

WARNING! DANGER! THESE NOTES CONTAIN A FATE WORSE THAN DEATH. STOP READING AND BURN THEM. BURN THIS NOTE. BURN IT. BURN.

The seventeenth note reads;

Scientists have been successful in containing the entity. The notes connected to its activity, however, are still known to appear occasionally, though it appears they have been rendered harmless. People still forget that the entity is real when not looking directly at it and staff in charge of containing it must regularly remind each other of its existence in order to continue successfully containing it. It is vital that the containment procedure is recorded and preserved, and the responsibility of containment be passed on from generation to generation. The containment procedure is [REDACTED].

The eighteenth note reads;

Burn.

The last paragraph of the nineteenth note reads;

This collection of notes is itself a note, the nineteenth note, the final note. It also contains all previous notes and may appear on its own. The nineteenth note acts as the full sequence of notes on its own. Thus, if you are reading this note, the sequence is complete.
