Set the entity and the notes on fire simultaneously, keep the blood-salt circles intact during this time if possible, keep them fresh around the ashes for at least a month. Do not scatter them. Sometimes, the final nineteenth note disappears and reappears within its containment unit, double check that it is present in the folder alongside the rest of the notes at time of destruction and absolutely do not attempt destruction unless it is there. If the procedure is not followed exactly, the entity will be released from its constraints.

Containment Protocol: Entity is to be kept in a standard cell containment unit, with the addition of a barrier lined with silver and smeared with a mixture of human blood and sea salt. There should be several circles drawn using the same mixture of blood and salt surrounding the entity in its cell, at least three at all times, thus, the size of the cell should accommodate this. The notes are to be contained in a folder in a locked tin container on a desk in a standard cell containment unit and the tin box is to be surrounded by a circle drawn in the same mixture of blood and salt, one circle may be drawn on the desk itself, and another should be drawn on the floor around the desk. The circles of blood and salt should be washed away and replaced roughly twice a week, both the ones surrounding the entity and the ones surrounding the notes.

Capture Protocol: Find the first note. Confine the reader in whatever location intended to contain the entity. Draw at least three circles of blood and salt surrounding them. Ensure that within the three circles they have enough amenities to sustain them for about two weeks to a month. Someone may enter and exit the circled barriers freely for resupplying purposes or medical interventions, as long as the circles remain carefully undamaged and the reader does not exit them at all. Wait for the rest of the notes to appear. Have the reader read them. Repeat this until all notes have appeared and been read. Confiscate each note after it is read. Do not allow anyone else to read them and immediately place them in a previously prepared separate containment unit for them including two blood salt circles drawn around whatever they will be contained in. Ensure that they will be secure and difficult to reach. After the last note has been read, wait for the entity to appear. It will not be able to leave. The reader will die.

Short Description: Faceless humanoid figure that causes extreme bodily harm, disappearance, and death to individuals within a two (2) kilometer (km) radius. It is accompanied by a series of notes that are highly likely to cause similar effects, especially to whomever has read them, unless ignored completely for a period of about one (1) month.

It is accompanied by a series of notes that are highly likely to cause similar effects, especially to whomever has read them, unless ignored completely for a period of about one (1) month.

Detailed Description: Entity has anatomy roughly resembling the human body but with key major differences. Has the same basic musculoskeletal structure and function as a human body. Lacks any facial features. Lacks skin texture and traits. Lacks any orifices, even pores. Lacks any internal organs. Has blood vessels. Has major veins and arteries that differ in size and placement compared to typical human anatomy. Has a nerve net rather than a central nervous system.

Proximity to the entity for any length of time is guaranteed to cause disappearance within the next twenty-four to thirty-six (24-36) hours for individuals under the age of fifteen, and is highly likely to still have the same result for individuals above the age of fourteen. In cases where disappearance doesn't occur, those who have encountered the entity are highly likely to spontaneously develop either minor, or major physical injuries that may be fatal. The entity is also known to induce sudden death.

The entity appears at seemingly random intervals and locations, most often in areas with at least some woodland, usually among groups of four (4) to twelve (12) people. Usually appears among shadows behind someone else. Any attempt to acquire footage of the entity results in corrupted files that do capture its vague general form but can plausibly and easily be dismissed as glitches or equipment malfunction.

There is a series of nineteen notes that are associated with the entity. They appear in places where one might typically find paragraphs of text, such as flyers, notes left in library books, anonymous posts on the internet, sticky notes on appliances, flyers on corkboards, and so on. The letters can appear either printed or handwritten. The handwriting seen in the notes is inconsistent and unlikely to have been written by the same person. Just like the entity itself, the notes may cause injury, death or disappearance, though requiring much more time and are much less likely to result in such outcomes. However, they almost guarantee an encounter with the entity itself for individuals who read them. The notes appear and disappear and reappear according to a specific algorithm.

If the first note is ignored, after some time, usually several days, it will disappear and reappear somewhere else at a later time, usually hours after its disappearance. If it is read, the second note in the sequence will appear somewhere within reach of the individual who read the first note, but it will be almost impossible for anyone who hasn't read the first note to perceive its existence, and even if they do perceive its existence, they will likely only notice it for a few seconds at most, and will probably see it as blank. If it is read the third note in the sequence will appear, only to the person who's read the previous notes in the sequence, and will only be perceptible to them. If it is ignored, it will disappear and the first note will reappear somewhere else, beginning the cycle anew, and this will happen with regards to any of the subsequent pages as well.

The nineteenth note often will not show up at all and the sequence is considered complete without it. Alternatively, it sometimes appears on its own and has a significantly lower chance to induce the same kind of effect as reading the previous eighteen notes.

If all eighteen notes are read, the reader is guaranteed to either encounter the entity within the next thirty-six (36) hours, or disappear within the next week. This is less likely to occur if the individual has only read some of the notes in the sequence. If the first note is ignored, it is incredibly unlikely that anyone who was in the presence of the first note will ever encounter the entity at all.
