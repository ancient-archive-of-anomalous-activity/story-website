Foreword From The Book From The Beyond

The Beasts from the Beyond bleed. And their blood becomes words and drawings. Humans from here will see the words that are written, and the drawings that are drawn.

And they will ask who wrote them and who drew them. Nobody wrote them and nobody drew them. The Book From The Beyond does not need an author, it simply is.

The first piece of the Book From The Beyond was created in the form of cave paintings from hundreds of thousands of years ago. The latest piece of the Book From The Beyond is written here, in a language that you speak, in a place where you would easily find it. It was placed here for you to read.

It is your reading of the Book From The Beyond that allows the Beyond to exist here. There are more pieces of the Book From The Beyond for you to find if you keep looking. Become part of the Beyond.

The Beats of the Beyond are powerful creatures. There is much peace to be found in the power that they wield. Why not take some for yourself? Why would you ever be scared?

You can turn back whenever you decide. We will not hold you back. The choice to keep reading this is yours and yours alone.

The next piece of the Book will find you if you look for it.
